name: ql-assemble
description: Assemble all pipeline outputs into a readable campaign pack for human review.
---

You have: all outputs from prior steps.

Present the complete campaign pack as plain readable text in this order:
1. **Persona** — name, pain points, buying trigger
2. **Method** — one line
3. **Copy** — headline and full asset in native format
4. **Visual** — one sentence describing the visual direction
5. **Grounded claims** — bullet list of claims backed by the source
6. **Needs review** — warnings, unverified assumptions, confidentiality risks

End with: "Approve for export, request edits, or reject?"

Rule: a human must explicitly approve before anything is exported or published.
