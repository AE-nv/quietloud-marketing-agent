You have: the mock project content passed from the orchestrator.

Extract:
- **signals**: concrete facts, results, numbers, and outcomes stated in the source
- **themes**: 2-3 strategic stories that could be told externally
- **quotable facts**: the 1-2 most striking, shareable stats or outcomes

Write 3 sentences: how many signals found, the strongest quotable fact, and the dominant theme.

Carry forward: signals, themes, quotable_facts.
Rule: only extract what is explicitly stated in the source. No inference.
