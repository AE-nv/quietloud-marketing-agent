# QuietLoud — Internal Positioning Note

Last updated: May 2026

## What QuietLoud does

QuietLoud is a B2B marketing agency specialising in demand generation for technology companies in the Belgian and Dutch market. We work with scale-ups (typically 30–300 employees) that have a product that sells but limited marketing infrastructure.

Core services:
- Demand generation campaigns (LinkedIn, email, content, webinar)
- Founder-led content strategy
- Campaign measurement and pipeline attribution

## Our sweet spot client

- B2B SaaS or tech-enabled services
- 30–300 employees
- Strong product, weak brand
- Active sales team but insufficient inbound
- Belgian or Dutch market, sometimes expanding to DACH or Nordics
- Budget: €5k–€25k/month
- Buyer: CMO, Head of Marketing, or founder acting as CMO

## What makes us different

We don't do brand campaigns. We build pipeline. Every engagement is measured against MQLs, pipeline influenced, or closed revenue where we have visibility.

We are specialists in the "zero to pipeline" problem: a company that has never done structured demand gen and needs to build it from scratch without a large team or budget.

## Current positioning gap

We have delivered strong results for several clients in 2025–2026 but have not systematically turned those into external proof. We have case studies in draft form but no consistent campaign around our own playbook. This is a known internal gap.
