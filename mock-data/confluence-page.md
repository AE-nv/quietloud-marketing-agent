# Project Delivery Note — Datalink Growth Campaign

**Client:** Datalink (B2B SaaS, 80-person scale-up, Brussels)
**Project type:** Demand generation campaign
**Campaign period:** Q1 2026
**QuietLoud team:** Saar V. (strategy), Robbe D. (content), Nina T. (performance)

---

## What we did

Datalink needed pipeline. Their sales team was closing well but inbound was near zero. No brand presence in the Belgian tech market, minimal content, no community touchpoints.

We ran a 10-week demand gen campaign targeting mid-market ops and finance teams at Belgian scale-ups (50–250 employees). The angle: "your ops stack is holding you back" — positioning Datalink's integration layer as the thing that unblocks growth without a big IT project.

**Channels used:**
- LinkedIn organic (3x per week cadence, founder-led)
- LinkedIn paid (retargeting + lookalike on existing customers)
- Two-part email nurture sequence triggered by content download
- One live webinar ("Hidden cost of disconnected tools") — 140 registrants, 89 attended

**Content produced:**
- 12 LinkedIn posts (founder voice)
- 1 lead magnet: "Ops Stack Audit Checklist" (PDF, 420 downloads)
- 2 email sequences (6 emails total)
- 1 webinar deck + recording
- 3 short case study snippets (anonymised)

---

## Results

| Metric | Target | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| MQLs generated | 40 | 67 |
| Webinar registrants | 80 | 140 |
| Checklist downloads | 200 | 420 |
| LinkedIn follower growth | +300 | +510 |
| Pipeline influenced (est.) | €180k | €310k |

---

## What worked

- Founder-led LinkedIn outperformed paid by 3:1 on engagement rate
- The checklist positioned Datalink as a thinking partner, not a vendor
- Webinar format created natural sales handoff — 18 of 89 attendees booked a follow-up call

## What didn't work

- Email open rates were lower than expected on sequence 2 (re-examine subject lines)
- Paid targeting on job title alone too broad — refined to include company size signals mid-campaign

## Lessons learned

- The "ops stack holding you back" angle resonated most with finance leads, not ops leads as expected
- Belgian B2B audiences respond to peer validation (customer quotes) much more than feature lists
- Short-form content (under 200 words) consistently outperformed long posts

---

## QuietLoud POV

This campaign confirmed a repeatable playbook for scale-ups entering the Belgian market without brand equity:
1. Start with a diagnostic asset (checklist, audit, maturity model)
2. Founder voice on LinkedIn as primary reach engine
3. Webinar as the pipeline conversion event
4. Tight ICP (50–250, ops or finance buyer) outperforms broad spray

We can replicate this for other integration or ops-tooling clients.
